## 2026-08-18 - Missing ARIA Labels on Icon-Only Controls
**Learning:** Icon-only interactive elements like photo carousels (chevron arrows and dot indicators) and modal close buttons ('X' icons) consistently lack accessible names (aria-label) across the application, making them unusable for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always verify and enforce the addition of aria-label attributes when implementing or reviewing icon-only buttons, carousels, or modal overlays to ensure baseline accessibility.
