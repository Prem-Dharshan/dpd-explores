## 2024-05-14 - Accessibility for Custom Photo Carousels and Modals
**Learning:** Icon-only controls such as carousel arrows (`<ChevronLeft />`, `<ChevronRight />`), indicator dots, and modal close buttons (`<X />`) are completely invisible to screen readers without explicit `aria-label`s. Furthermore, custom carousels and modals often lack focus styles for keyboard users, making keyboard navigation confusing or impossible.
**Action:** Actively scan for custom UI patterns (like carousels or modals) and always ensure all interactive elements (buttons, indicator dots) have descriptive `aria-label` attributes and explicit `focus-visible` utility classes (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-accent focus-visible:outline-none`) to support robust keyboard accessibility.
