## 2024-09-02 - Custom Carousel & Modal Accessibility
**Learning:** Custom interactive components like photo carousels and modals often lack native semantic buttons or keyboard support for their controls. Specifically, indicator dots and modal close buttons are frequently missed for keyboard users if they lack proper `focus-visible` styling and descriptive `aria-label`s, which are crucial for screen reader context when visual labels are absent.
**Action:** When working on custom carousels or modals, always verify that every interactive element (like previous/next arrows, pagination dots, and close buttons) has a clear `aria-label` describing its function (e.g., "Go to photo 1"). Additionally, ensure they have explicit focus styles (like `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-accent`) so they are discoverable via keyboard navigation.
