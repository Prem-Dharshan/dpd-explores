## 2024-08-25 - Add keyboard focus and ARIA labels to custom carousel and modal controls
**Learning:** Icon-only interactive elements in custom carousels (prev/next buttons, indicator dots) and modals (close buttons) often lack built-in accessibility. Without explicit `aria-label`s, screen readers cannot convey their purpose, and without explicit focus styling (`focus-visible`), keyboard users cannot perceive when they have focus.
**Action:** When implementing or modifying custom interactive components like carousels and modals, ensure all icon-only buttons receive descriptive `aria-label` attributes and explicit focus indicator styles (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-accent focus-visible:outline-none`) to guarantee both screen reader clarity and keyboard navigation visibility.
